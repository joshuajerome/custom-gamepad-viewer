Custom Gamepad Viewer

Add overlay.html as a Browser Source in Streamlabs or OBS. Press a button on your controller once so the browser can see it.

  • White theme, player 1
  • Black theme, player 1
  • White theme with input debug
  • White theme, trigger meter mode

URL options

  • theme: white or black
  • p: player slot 1 to 4
  • scale: fit (default) or a number such as 0.5
  • dz: stick deadzone, default 0.25
  • offset: stick travel in pixels, default 22
  • tilt=0: turn off stick tilt
  • trigger=meter: fill triggers from the bottom instead of fading in
  • debug=1: show raw controller input
